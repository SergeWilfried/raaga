# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Procurement and maintenance staff at mines and industrial plants in West Africa (Burkina Faso, Guinea and neighbours), plus resellers who buy in volume. They are often on site, on a phone or a weak connection, and usually arrive with a part number or an equipment model in hand. Many read French first, English second.

## Product Purpose

A B2B catalog and ordering storefront for surplus and slow-moving mining spare parts, sold on behalf of several client companies. Buyers find a part, see stock and a pre-tax price, add it to a cart (VAT is calculated there) or request a quote when no price is listed. Success is a buyer reaching the right part number in a few steps and trusting the stock, brand and price they see. The home page does some brand work, but the catalog and cart are the product.

## Brand Personality

Direct, reliable, industrial. Plain-spoken and dependable, built for people who work with heavy equipment. No flourish, no marketing gloss; information first.

## Anti-references

- A cluttered legacy parts catalogue: dense, unstyled tables with no hierarchy or spacing.
- The default Medusa starter: the out-of-the-box look that is recognisably a template.
- Client company names ("Waghion", "Nordgold") must never appear in customer-facing navigation or labels.

## Design Principles

1. **Part number first.** Search and filters get a buyer to the right item faster than anything else on the page; everything else supports that.
2. **Say what you know, plainly.** Show brand, stock and pre-tax price clearly; say "price on request" instead of leaving a gap.
3. **Short menus, deep search.** Navigation shows a few busy categories with a "See more" link; the long tail lives in search and filters.
4. **Built for site conditions.** Works on a phone, on a slow connection, in bright light: large targets, high contrast, light pages.
5. **Two languages, one layout.** French and English copy both fit without breaking the interface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: text contrast of 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text), full keyboard operation, visible focus, screen-reader labels, and reduced motion respected. All copy must work in French and English.
