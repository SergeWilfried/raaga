# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Two sides of one local market.

- **Buyers:** procurement and maintenance staff at mines and other industrial sites (quarries, plants, power, construction) in West Africa, plus resellers who buy in volume. They are often on site, on a phone or a weak connection, and usually arrive with a part number or an equipment model in hand. Many read French first, English second.
- **Sellers:** mines and industrial sites holding surplus or slow-moving spare parts they want to turn into cash instead of storing.

## Product Purpose

A local marketplace for spare parts held at mines and industrial sites in West Africa. Buyers find a part, see stock and a pre-tax price, add it to a cart (VAT is calculated there) or request a quote when no price is listed. Sourcing from another site in the region means shorter lead times and less freight and logistics than ordering from overseas. Sellers send their parts list as a sheet; Raaga uploads it to the shop, and payment is arranged with the seller by hand. Success is a buyer reaching the right part number in a few steps, trusting the stock, brand and price, and a seller seeing a clear route to sell.

## Brand Personality

Direct, reliable, industrial. Plain-spoken and dependable, built for people who work with heavy equipment. No flourish, no marketing gloss; information first.

## Anti-references

- A cluttered legacy parts catalogue: dense, unstyled tables with no hierarchy or spacing.
- The default Medusa starter: the out-of-the-box look that is recognisably a template.
- Client company names ("Waghion", "Nordgold") must never appear in customer-facing navigation or labels.

## Design Principles

1. **Local is the advantage.** Say plainly that the stock is already in the region: shorter lead times, less freight. Never invent numbers for the savings.
2. **Part number first.** Search and filters get a buyer to the right item faster than anything else on the page; everything else supports that.
3. **Say what you know, plainly.** Show brand, stock and pre-tax price clearly; say "price on request" instead of leaving a gap.
4. **Short menus, deep search.** Navigation shows a few busy categories with a "See more" link; the long tail lives in search and filters.
5. **Built for site conditions.** Works on a phone, on a slow connection, in bright light: large targets, high contrast, light pages.
6. **Two languages, one layout.** French and English copy both fit without breaking the interface.
7. **Two doors, one front page.** Buying leads; selling is always one clear step away. Never promise self-service listing or payout terms that are not built.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: text contrast of 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text), full keyboard operation, visible focus, screen-reader labels, and reduced motion respected. All copy must work in French and English.
